## Pitch
A repo-local skill suite and CLI that let people choose software outcomes while agents research, plan, implement, and maintain the knowledge needed to continue.

## Who it's for
A solo builder and agents working across coding harnesses, including interactive sessions and future unattended pipelines.

## Why
Each completed change should leave the next agent a clear account of current behavior, useful next work, and the constraints that matter.

## Constraints
Humans choose outcomes and constraints; agents research, plan, and execute within them. Keep knowledge in the repository and routine context compact. Preserve actual human judgments where acceptance requires them. Process scheduling and durable attempts belong behind an optional executor interface.

## Not now
A central application/database, compulsory issue tracker, mandatory agent roster, full Bench migration, or claims of unattended reliability without an exercised recovery path.
